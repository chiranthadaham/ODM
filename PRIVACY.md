# Open Download Manager Extension Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

This policy describes how the Open Download Manager (ODM) browser extension handles information when connecting your browser to the separately installed ODM desktop application.

## Information the extension uses

To detect and transfer downloads, the extension may access:

- Download URLs and suggested filenames.
- The source website or referring page address associated with a download.
- Detected video and audio links.
- Request and response information needed to identify and process downloads, such as content types and file sizes.

URLs may contain personal information or temporary access tokens supplied by the website.

## How information is used

The extension uses this information to identify downloadable content, display detected media, and send downloads to the ODM desktop application.

The extension communicates with ODM locally through the browser's native messaging system. The desktop application then contacts the relevant websites or download servers to retrieve the requested content. Those services receive information associated with the download request, including your IP address, and handle it under their own policies.

## Sharing and tracking

The extension does not send information to an ODM-operated analytics or advertising service. It does not sell personal information or include advertising or analytics tracking.

Download information is passed to the locally installed ODM application to provide the requested functionality. The extension does not request access to your browser's stored passwords, browsing-history database, or cookies.

## Storage and retention

The extension stores preferences and your browser-integration consent choice in browser extension storage. Information about detected downloads and media is held temporarily in memory.

The desktop application may retain download queue entries, source URLs, filenames, progress information, and downloaded files on your computer. Removing the browser extension does not automatically remove desktop application data or downloaded files.

## Your choices

Browser integration requires your consent. You can disable automatic capture or revoke integration consent through the extension.

You can remove the extension through your browser's extension manager. You can manage download records and downloaded files through the desktop application and your operating system.

## Private browsing

The extension is configured not to operate in private or incognito browsing windows.

## Security

Communication between the extension and ODM uses the browser's native messaging mechanism. Keep your browser and desktop application updated, and download ODM only from trusted project distribution channels.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or information handling changes. The updated policy will show a revised date. Any newly required consent will be requested through the extension or browser.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the [ODM project Issues page](https://github.com/chiranthadaham/ODM/issues). Issues are public: do not include passwords, private download URLs, access tokens, or other sensitive personal information.
